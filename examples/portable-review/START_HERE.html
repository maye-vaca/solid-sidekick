Review a CAD revision in your browser

This is an illustrative display riser: two plates and four spacers. Revision A has a 32 mm gap between the plates. Revision B changes only that gap to 48 mm, extending the spacers and moving the upper plate. Plate width, depth and thickness stay unchanged.

No assistant, Python, installation, server or internet connection is needed to view this example. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder first; do not open it inside the ZIP preview. Use an ordinary browser. Microsoft Edge on Windows ARM64 is the browser exercised for this release; other browsers and platforms are not yet qualified.

Try the complete review loop

  1. Open review.html once. Its current revision should be initial (A). The prior-reference panel is initially empty.
  2. Use Compare isometric, drag the model to rotate it, and optionally uncheck a component in the current view. Leave this review tab open.
  3. In that same review tab, use Load newer review and choose updates\revision-b.json from your extracted folder. The message should say gap-48 is waiting. The displayed revision must still be initial; loading is not applying.
  4. When you are ready, choose Apply newer revision yourself. The current revision should become gap-48, with A shown as the prior reference. Your camera and matching component visibility should be retained. The model must not change before this click.

Applying a display update is not manufacturing approval. These files are snapshots, not a live connection to CAD. The review page labels geometry unchecked at export: production checks were performed separately, but the page does not certify current files, physical fit, strength or safety.

If something goes wrong

Keep the current page open and report the visible message. Do not weaken browser security or organization policy. A browser without suitable WebGL or local-file support may not be usable for this trial.

After B is displayed, reloading the original A HTML may intentionally block the stale snapshot. Load the same B packet and choose Apply again to recover the last-shown revision. This protection is local to the page's browser session, not a global registry across browsers or a publisher signature.

The page checks packet consistency and lineage. Per-file hashes in file-hashes.json provide an inventory, not proof of publisher identity. No remote file URLs or original developer folders are needed.

Feedback to return

Report your browser/OS, whether A opened, whether B stayed pending until your click, whether your view was preserved, and any error. Automated checks and your human review are recorded separately.
