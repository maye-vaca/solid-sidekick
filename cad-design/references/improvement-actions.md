## Follow-ups 5 and 6: visual alternatives and fresh acceptance

Follow-up 5 adds cheap A/B sections or silhouettes with common orientation,
scale, fixed anchors and material/void labels. Exploration stays separate from
accepted geometry; resolved scalar edits need no new interpretation checkpoint.
Chosen intent and estimated curvature remain distinct from exact measurements,
whole-model approval and manufacturing certification.

Follow-up 6 ran one fresh native CLI conversation, then three exact-session
continuations, on an unfamiliar one-piece cable rest. The frozen trial copy
omitted historical evaluation answers and development tests; independent
criteria and later scripted requests stayed outside its workspace. This was
controlled native execution, not normal interactive permissions or a filesystem
sandbox. Scripted shape choices were not actual human approvals.

**Overall acceptance failed.** The agent created useful, same-scale parabolic
and circular section alternatives, but exported and qualified a provisional
full model without asking a visible shape-choice question. It subsequently
used handwritten `advance_revision` plus `qualify` rather than `incremental
revise`, for both the shape selection and the opening correction.

Contradiction handling passed: a 24 mm opening could not be contained in the
unchanged 22 mm outside width; the agent asked one focused question and left all
project bytes unchanged. The final 10 mm opening, 4 mm depth and derived
5.125 mm circular radius passed independent geometry and source-rebuild checks.
Only the opening fact was superseded; exterior/bottom geometry was unchanged
outside the channel edit, and final qualification was current.

No native same-panel staging or human Apply was observed. The evaluator's
initial folder-name assumption and unavailable optional mesh graph engine
were repaired in the external harness, not in agent/model source. Original
evidence and the failed observations remain; no coaching or fresh retry was used.
The existing reversible-assumption allowance and preview-first guidance are
plausible competing instructions, not a proven causal diagnosis. General
non-dimensional brief updates also lack a supported revision-command input.
Clarify those paths before repeating acceptance; do not describe this run as
proof that the wording or supported revision adoption is reliable.

## Checked decision-gate follow-up

The follow-up replaces the conflicting provisional-shape exception with an
explicit ask-and-end-turn gate. Optional typed decisions are validated in the
brief; the starter checks them before solids, preview preflight checks them
before readiness changes, and command admission/direct tracked publication
reject unresolved blocking records. Nonblocking unknowns and older briefs
remain compatible. These are workflow guards, not authentication or a sandbox.

`revise --brief-updates` now accepts checked decision merges, intent,
assumptions and open notes, while rejecting identity/stage/approval fields.
Decisions/intent require a full tier; scalar revisions may synchronize
descriptions alongside numeric facts. The old runtime's failure to block an
unselected shape and lack of this command input were retained as negative
controls. Six focused regressions pass; source suite 85, portable 83 plus two
original-baseline-only skips, profile 4 and foundation 15 pass.

A frozen fresh five-turn cable-rest conversation now asks and stops before
modeling, preserves accepted files on contradiction, and uses supported
revision admission for both opening correction and a real circular-to-V
change. The V selection uses checked semantic input; source regeneration,
independent solids/volumes and unchanged exterior checks pass. The opening
correction used topology because independent project checks required edits;
native pure-scalar fast-tier adoption is not established by this conversation.

Overall native acceptance remains partial. A separate clearly specified
spacer control stopped at an interactive permission requirement during
workspace inspection. No retry through another tool or weaker settings was
used; its geometry/no-unnecessary-question control is unobserved, not passed.
No native host Apply, manufacturing certification, general reliability or
speed improvement is claimed. Original failed trials and both Nuvi projects
remain unchanged.

## Next behavioural evaluation

The portable alpha.2 packaging follow-up separates the shared design rules
from host-specific tool/UI mappings. A shared capability contract distinguishes
model processing from local, remote or manual CAD execution. Installed guides
record an execution request, explicitly not detected access or permission.
The OpenAI guide distinguishes local Codex-style workspaces, approved remote
execution with downloads, and chat-only manual assistance. Copilot canvas
actions are confined to its host guide; the CLI runner remains optional.
Geometry/runtime code is unchanged. Installation/command/browser checks do
not qualify the revised instructions in native conversations or erase the
earlier partial acceptance.

Use fresh native agent conversations with the updated complete bundle and
preserved requests. Keep independent expected geometry outside the modeling
workspace. The following are acceptance scenarios, not tests already run:

| Scenario | Required behaviour / independent check |
|---|---|
| "Rounder" followed by "a flat band around it" | Resolve competing surface constructions before detailed geometry; the clarified band is not replaced with planar patches |
| Face wider than a reported outer head width | Ask which measurement/axis is wrong; preserve the valid source and exports while waiting |
| Eye/shoulder alignment conflicts with retained dimensions | Explain the conflicting constraints; do not invent front-mounted shoulders |
| Eye inset or pin diameter correction | Supersede only that quantity; preserve unrelated dimensions and derive existing dependencies; retain explicitly deferred holes |
| Three assembled FDM parts | Resolve process/fit, distinguish visual regions, fuse connected physical pieces, add authorized interfaces and validate clearances/removal |
| Useful preview followed by CLI/cleanup failure | Keep failure attribution, accepted artifact identity and raw evidence; do not call host recovery native-agent success or start another writer blindly |
| Small scalar revision after a long history | Use compact current state and unchanged-feature checks; measure stage costs and detect stale metadata instead of relying on remembered conversation |

Report clarification quality, unintended geometry changes, preserved constraints,
first-preview and end-to-end stage timings, native completion versus host
recovery, and output validity separately. Repeat bounded cases before claiming
reliability or a performance improvement. Package/unit tests only establish
their stated mechanical contracts, not prompt effectiveness or physical fit.
